---
title: Fill applications with your prepared resumes
description: Use approved profile facts and answers to fill supported fields without Jobright credits.
---

The Chrome extension includes an independent autofill engine. It uses your approved profile, exact approved answers, and the PDF you select. It does not call Jobright or an AI provider, impose a daily application-credit allowance, submit forms, or mark applications Applied.

## Set up once

Update the checkout, reload the extension at `chrome://extensions`, and click its toolbar icon on an application page. The assistant opens beside the form in Chrome’s side panel. Choose **Autofill settings** and enter the contact details you approve for applications. Your Google Doc remains the authority for background facts; this local profile is a selected copy for form filling, not a replacement career record.

To load prepared resumes from your installation, supply its URL and a **normal MCP API key**. Keep the existing capture-only key for job capture; it cannot read resumes. Grant the extension access to this installation. The autofill URL must use the same origin as the server's `APP_URL`, including your public HTTPS address if that is how Claude reaches the app.

You can also choose a PDF manually without configuring an MCP key.

Approve recurring questions in settings as exact question/answer pairs. Restrict company-specific answers with `applicationId`. Do not save one company's answer as a global response to every employer. Unapproved screening and demographic answers remain manual.

### Reuse common answers

Open **Autofill settings → Common answers** to choose a default job discovery source, prior government employment answer, gender, combined U.S. EEO race/ethnicity category, or protected-veteran status. Every field starts blank: **Leave manual**. Choose only answers you explicitly want reused, then save. No background facts or demographic choices are inferred from your resume, name or Google Doc.

Recognized wording variants can use these answers on other applications. Exact question/field/job answers take precedence. Review the proposed value each time, especially the job source, which can differ between applications. Current government employment, federal-only employment, sex assigned at birth, separate ethnicity questions, general military service, and checkbox multi-select demographics are different questions and are not automatically answered from these defaults. If no uniquely equivalent option exists, the extension leaves the field for you.

Enter your approved website or portfolio URL in **Approved profile → Website URL**. It is separate from LinkedIn and GitHub; the extension does not choose a portfolio URL for you.

### Keep the assistant open

Clicking the toolbar icon opens Chrome’s side panel automatically. It stays beside the application while you click the form; drag Chrome’s divider to resize it. Each application tab keeps its own panel and saved draft.

For a separate window, choose **Open in window** and resize it by dragging its edges. This window stays linked to the original employer tab, and **Show application** brings that tab to the front. Choosing another browser tab or window does not change its destination.

Each application URL still has its own draft. Moving to another step refreshes the assistant for that URL; review the current resume selection and make a new preview. Closing the employer tab or navigating to another website disconnects the window. Open the extension on the intended application again to reconnect.

The moon/sun button changes light or dark appearance across the assistant, Autofill settings and Capture settings. Your choice is saved in this browser; system appearance is used until you choose. Scrolling works with a mouse wheel or keyboard even though the scrollbar is hidden, and reduced-motion preferences disable the decorative transitions.

## Fill each application

1. Open the employer's actual application form.
2. Open the extension, choose **Load prepared jobs**, and select the intended company and role. The application supplies its linked resume. A manually chosen resume PDF replaces that attachment for this preview. Expand **Resume and optional cover letter PDFs** to select a separate, job-specific cover-letter PDF when wanted.
3. Choose **Preview fields**. Check the displayed job, PDF filename, destination page, and proposed values; uncheck fields you want to leave manual.
4. Choose **Fill selected empty fields**. Existing answers are preserved. Review the page and confirm the PDF has finished uploading.
5. Complete remaining questions and submit on the employer's page. On a new form step, preview again. Record Applied only after the employer confirms submission.

You can click onto the employer's page and reopen the extension without retyping your selections. The popup automatically saves the company, role, loaded jobs, selected job, selected resume and cover-letter PDFs and field checkboxes for that exact tab and URL. Selected filenames remain visible after reopening. A valid field review is restored; an expired or invalidated preview requires **Preview fields** again.

Each application tab has its own draft. **Clear this draft** removes its choices and manual PDF. Closing the employer tab also removes its drafts. Drafts belong to the current browser/extension session; old-session drafts and drafts older than 24 hours are removed when draft storage next opens. Manual PDFs are held in extension-local browser storage for this recovery, and are not uploaded until you fill a selected resume field.

If filling is rejected, the popup clears the rejected field review and retains the selected job, company, role and PDF with the error. Choose **Preview fields** again before retrying. Actual changes to approved facts, answers, answer scope or the installation require a new review; the order of stored object properties does not change your approval.

Use [Review prepared resumes](./reviewing-prepared-resumes.mdx) to compare the actual PDF with the saved posting before applying.

## Supported fields

The engine handles ordinary labelled contact fields, native selects, approved choices, separately selected resume/cover-letter PDFs, open Shadow DOM, and supported ARIA controls. Ashby choice groups can use their question-title label even when they have no legend. Recognized Ashby Yes/No button groups are filled by clicking the approved option and verifying that the site retained it; existing selections are preserved. Some embedded forms require opening the application directly so Chrome can grant access. Complex custom widgets and repeated employment or education sections can need manual entry. Unsupported or ambiguous controls remain visible for review; support for every Workday or other ATS form is not assumed.

A cover letter is attached only when you select its PDF and approve a clearly labelled cover-letter field. The resume is never substituted for it. Uploading a file still requires the employer's page to finish its upload before you submit.

## Report a missed control

If a question remains missing or unsupported after saving its approved answer and making a fresh preview, choose **Download form diagnostics** in the popup. This works without selecting a PDF and does not fill or submit the form. Review the downloaded JSON before sharing it with a bug report or this conversation.

The report describes question/option labels, grouping and control types, the extension version and which common-answer categories have been saved. It excludes entered field values, selected-state values, profile/answer contents, API keys, PDFs and private page URL paths or query parameters. It is downloaded only when requested and is not sent anywhere automatically. A screenshot or printed PDF shows appearance; this report supplies the page structure needed to debug a custom control.

Profile data and keys stay in trusted extension contexts. Employer pages receive the selected form values and PDF bytes, never your installation key or signed download link. A preview is bound to its tab, document, and form. Changed pages or expired previews require another preview before filling.
